## Overview

A no-expense-spared, frame-off restomod of a numbers-matching-body 1969 Corvette
Stingray T-top coupe. Over $164,000 invested and 2,400 shop hours across an
18-month build, completed in 2025. Fewer than 1,300 miles since completion.
Every mechanical system is new or fully rebuilt — this car drives like a modern
performance car and turns heads like nothing modern can.

  panels and console
