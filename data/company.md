## Who We Are

Ironwood Custom Classics has been building and selling custom American classics
for 30 years. Founded in 1996 by master fabricator Ray Delgado in Pasadena,
California, the shop started as a two-bay garage restoring Corvettes and
first-generation Camaros. Today Ironwood operates a 22,000-square-foot facility
with a team of 14 — fabricators, painters, upholsterers, and an in-house
electrical specialist — and has delivered more than 480 completed builds to
clients in 44 states and 9 countries.

## What We Do

- **Full frame-off restomods** — classic bodies over modern drivetrains,
  suspension, and electronics. Our specialty for three decades.
- **Concours-correct restorations** — for collectors who want the car exactly
  as it left the factory.
- **Curated sales** — every car we sell was either built in our shop or passed
  our 150-point inspection. We do not sell projects, and we do not sell
  surprises.

## Our History

- **1996** — Ray Delgado opens Ironwood in a two-bay garage in Pasadena,
  restoring Corvettes and first-generation Camaros.
- **2003** — First national Best in Class award, for a frame-off 1957 Bel Air.
- **2009** — Moves into the current 22,000-square-foot facility with dedicated
  paint booth and chassis shop.
- **2015** — Brings upholstery and electrical fully in-house; every part of a
  build now happens under one roof.
- **2018** — Ray's daughter Marisol Delgado takes over as general manager;
  Ray still leads fabrication.
- **2021** — Launches the international sales program; first overseas
  deliveries to Japan and Germany.
- **2026** — Celebrating 30 years and more than 480 completed builds.

## Notable Builds

- **"Midnight Ember"** — a 1967 Mustang fastback restomod that appeared on
  screen in a streaming television drama; still our most-requested build
  sheet.
- **"The Silver Bullet"** — a 1963 split-window Corvette concours restoration
  that took top honors at a national Corvette show.
- **"Hammerhead"** — a 900-horsepower pro-touring 1970 Chevelle SS built for a
  professional athlete; magazine cover car.
- **"La Paloma"** — a 1959 Cadillac Eldorado convertible restored for a
  classic-car museum collection in Southern California.

## Worldwide Sales & Shipping

We sell and ship worldwide — more than 40 of our cars live outside the United
States today, across 9 countries including Japan, Germany, the UK, Australia,
and the UAE.

- **Domestic:** fully insured, enclosed door-to-door transport anywhere in the
  US and Canada.
- **International:** enclosed container sea freight or air freight, arranged
  through our export partners. We handle the export paperwork, title work, and
  customs documentation; typical door-to-door time is 4–8 weeks by sea.
- **Remote buying:** live video walkarounds, cold-start and driving videos,
  independent pre-purchase inspections welcome, and secure escrow payment for
  international transactions.

## Our Promise

- **Documentation first.** Every car sells with its complete build history,
  receipts, and dyno results. If we can't document it, we don't claim it.
- **12-month / 12,000-mile limited drivetrain warranty** on every Ironwood
  build we sell.
- **No-pressure process.** Pre-purchase inspections welcome, video walkarounds
  on request, and we'll answer every question honestly — including what a car
  is *not*.
